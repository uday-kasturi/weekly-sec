## The situation

Second person: who you are, when it is, and what just happened. If an alert fired, quote it in a code block. The framing is the first, free hint: a careful reader should come away knowing where to start.

## Your mission

What your lead or client wants, and by when. Map it to the objectives in plain words.

## The environment you're defending

The systems, identities and data involved, with what is *normal* for each. Use tables. Players can't spot abnormal behaviour without knowing what normal looks like.

## The evidence

Each log `source` and the fields that matter. Each evidence file and what it is. The time range.

## Field notes

The two to five concepts this case depends on, explained just enough that a newcomer can reason about them without giving away the answers. Example: how temporary credentials work, what a field means.

## How to work

Where to start in the game, and how the answers, hints, board, notebook and **End investigation** button work.
